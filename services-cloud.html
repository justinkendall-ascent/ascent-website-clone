Cloud-Hosted Service

From Touchdown to Takeoff™

Ascent Technology's cloud-hosted subscription service delivers the power of SmartAirline, SmartAirport, and WorkZone in a secure, reliable, scalable cloud-hosted environment — accessible from any standard browser on your network.

Get Started

Cloud Hosting Since 1998

Ascent Technology has provided best-in-class hosted solutions for mission-critical resource optimization and allocation from our managed data center since 1998 — long before cloud computing became mainstream — and from our cloud-hosted AWS environments since 2017.

Our From Touchdown to Takeoff service lets you access Ascent Technology's complete suite of solutions using a standard web browser directly from your network, with no need to install, maintain, or support on-premise hardware or software.

What's Included

  • Full SmartAirline Operations Center suite (if licensed)
  • Full SmartAirport Operations Center suite (if licensed)
  • Full Ascent WorkZone workforce-management suite (if licensed)
  • 24×7 monitoring and proactive infrastructure management
  • Regular software updates and patches — delivered automatically
  • Dedicated secure environment per customer
  • AWS infrastructure with high availability and disaster recovery
  • Full 24×7 technical support from our Boston team

No On-Premise Infrastructure Required

Subscribe to our cloud-hosted service and access the complete Ascent Technology solution suite using a standard browser — eliminating capital expenditure on servers, database licenses, and IT infrastructure management overhead.

Secure, Dedicated Environments

Each customer operates in a dedicated, isolated environment within AWS. Your data is never co-mingled with other customers, and all communications are encrypted in transit and at rest.

  • Uptime: High availability architecture
