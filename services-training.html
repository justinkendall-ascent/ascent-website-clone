Training Services

Training Courses

Ascent Technology offers seminar-style and small-group training courses for the SmartAirline Operations Center, SmartAirport Operations Center, and Ascent WorkZone workforce-management system.

Flexible Training Delivery

Ascent Technology's training courses are designed to help your team get up to speed quickly and use our solutions productively from day one. We offer flexible delivery options to accommodate your schedule, location, and learning preferences.

Training Delivery Options

Remote Training

Delivered over the web using video conferencing and screen sharing. Remote training is cost-effective and flexible, allowing participants to attend from anywhere in the world without travel expense.

On-Site Training

Ascent Technology trainers travel to your location to deliver training in your own operational environment. On-site training is particularly effective for large teams and can be customized to your specific workflows and configurations.

Boston Headquarters Training

Attend training at Ascent Technology's offices in the Financial District of downtown Boston, MA. Small-group sessions at our headquarters provide an intimate learning environment with direct access to our product experts.
