The Complete Airline Operations Solution

Air-transportation professionals worldwide rely on Ascent Technology's web-hosted SmartAirline Operations Center solution for coordinated planning, scheduling, optimization, and real-time management of human and physical resources.

The SmartAirline Operations Center solution contains four integrated product suites that coordinate the planning, scheduling, and real-time management of critical airline resources, activities, and events across your entire network.

Whether you are assigning aircraft to routes, managing gates and ground resources at your stations, or recovering from a disruption in real time, SmartAirline gives your operations team a single, integrated platform to make fast, confident decisions.

Integrated Product Suites

The SmartAirline Operations Center solution is organized into four integrated suites, each targeting a distinct phase of airline operations management:

SmartAirline Planning Manager

Long-range resource planning for aircraft routing, crew planning, and station resource requirements across your entire schedule.

SmartAirline Scheduling Manager

Builds optimized schedules for ground resources including gates, stands, check-in counters, baggage belts, ramp equipment, and departure lounges.

SmartAirline Operations Manager

Short-term planning and real-time management of key station resources with intelligent schedule recovery support when operations deviate from plan.

SmartAirline Reporting Manager

Comprehensive historical analysis and performance reporting across all resource types, activities, and operational metrics.

Resources Managed

SmartAirline Operations Center supports end-to-end management of the key physical and human resources that airlines depend on at every station:

  • Gates and aircraft stands
  • Remote parking positions
  • Check-in counters and desks
  • Baggage make-up and reclaim belts
  • Ramp equipment and tugs
  • Ground handling buses
  • Departure lounges and boarding gates
  • Ground crew and station staff
  • Aircraft lines of flight and tail assignments

Intelligent Schedule Recovery

When operations do not go according to plan — due to weather, mechanical issues, crew shortages, or other disruptions — SmartAirline provides intelligent real-time schedule-recovery support to minimize downstream impacts and get your operation back on track as quickly as possible.
