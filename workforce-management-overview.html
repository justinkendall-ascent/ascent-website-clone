Intelligent Workforce Management from Forecast to Payroll

The Ascent WorkZone® workforce-management system is the intelligent workforce-optimization, workforce-planning, workforce-scheduling, and workforce-management solution for mission-critical and labor-intensive environments — including airlines, airports, casinos, hospitals, hotels, resorts, restaurants, retail stores, and security operations.

Ascent WorkZone creates the best plans to meet your forecasted workload, turns optimal plans into schedules, assigns workers to schedules, adjusts schedules and assignments as workloads change, assigns tasks to workers in real time, and tracks time and attendance.

The WorkZone Product Suite

Ascent WorkZone is an integrated suite of specialized products, each handling a specific phase of the workforce management lifecycle:

Product Function
ARIS/WorkModelWorkload generator — forecasts workload requirements based on historical data and operational inputs
ARIS/WorkOptimizeWork period generator — creates optimal work periods to meet forecasted demand
ARIS/WorkPlanWork schedule generator — builds full schedules from work period templates
ARIS/WorkNetBid and trade manager — web-based self-service tool for schedule bidding and peer-to-peer trading
ARIS/WorkTimeWorkday manager — real-time management of daily worker assignments and attendance
ARIS/WorkRelayTask and attendance monitor — assigns tasks to workers in real time and tracks time and attendance

Key Capabilities

Workforce Optimization & Planning

Accurately forecast workload requirements and create optimized workforce plans that balance labor costs with service requirements — weeks or months in advance.

Schedule Building & Bidding

Generate conflict-free work schedules automatically. Worker-centric, web-enabled self-service tools let workers choose work and vacation schedules based on seniority and preferences.

Real-Time Task Assignment

Assign tasks to workers in real time as workloads fluctuate. Workers receive task assignments on mobile or fixed terminals, and supervisors see a live operations dashboard at all times.

Time & Attendance Tracking

Automated time-and-attendance monitoring eliminates manual timekeeping. Integration with payroll systems ensures accurate, on-time pay for all workers.
