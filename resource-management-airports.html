Airport Resource Management

SmartAirport Operations Center®

Forecast, plan, schedule, optimize, and manage your airport resources so you can operate your airport, handle your flights, and accommodate your customers efficiently and cost-effectively.

Request a Demo

Airport Operations, Optimized End-to-End

Ascent Technology's SmartAirport Operations Center solution provides airports with a single integrated platform for forecasting resource requirements, creating optimal resource plans and schedules, and managing resources and operations in real time.

The solution supports the short-term planning and real-time management of every key airport resource — from gates and check-in counters to ramp equipment and passenger buses — ensuring that the right resources are in the right place at the right time.

When operations deviate from plan — due to flight delays, equipment failures, or passenger volume surges — SmartAirport provides intelligent real-time schedule-recovery support to restore normal operations as quickly as possible.

Key Capabilities

Resource Forecasting & Planning

Accurately forecast future resource requirements based on flight schedules, historical demand patterns, and operational constraints. Create optimized resource deployment plans weeks or months in advance.

Schedule Optimization

Transform resource plans into detailed, conflict-free operational schedules. Automated optimization algorithms ensure maximum resource utilization while respecting all operational rules and constraints.

Real-Time Operations Management

Monitor live airport operations on a dynamic display that shows actual versus planned resource assignments. Intelligent alerts notify operators of developing conflicts and suggest corrective actions.

Schedule Recovery

When disruptions occur, the solution analyzes the downstream impact on all affected resources and flights, then recommends the optimal recovery plan to minimize delays and customer inconvenience.

Resources Managed

  • Gates and aircraft stands (contact and remote)
  • Remote parking positions
  • Check-in counters and service desks
  • Baggage make-up areas and reclaim belts
  • Ramp equipment — tugs, belt loaders, pushbacks
  • Passenger buses (apron transport)
  • Departure lounges and boarding areas
  • Airport staff and ground handlers
  • Customs and immigration facilities
