Technology Partners

IBM

Ascent Technology is an IBM Premier Business Partner. Ascent Technology has worked closely with IBM's Travel and Transportation team for nearly 40 years, combining Ascent's advanced resource allocation and optimization software with IBM's enterprise infrastructure and global reach.

Oracle

Ascent Technology is a Gold Level member of the Oracle PartnerNetwork. This partnership reflects Ascent Technology's commitment to delivering solutions built on proven, enterprise-grade database and platform technologies.

Honeywell International

Ascent Technology is a business partner of Honeywell International, integrating with Honeywell's airport and building management systems to deliver comprehensive operational solutions for airports worldwide.

Industry Affiliations

Ascent Technology is active in the air-transportation industry through membership and participation in key industry organizations:

  • ACI — Airports Council International
  • ACI-NA — Airports Council International – North America
