Implementation Services

Implementation & Deployment

Ascent Technology's structured implementation methodology delivers your solution on time and within budget — tailored to your specific operational environment, existing systems, and business requirements.

A Proven Three-Phase Approach

Ascent Technology has implemented resource optimization and workforce management solutions for clients worldwide for nearly 40 years. Our structured three-phase implementation process minimizes disruption to your operations while ensuring a successful deployment.

Phase 1 — Discovery & Planning

Our implementation team works with your stakeholders to understand your operational environment, existing processes, data sources, and integration requirements. We develop a detailed project plan, data migration strategy, and acceptance test plan.

Phase 2 — Configuration & Integration

We configure the Ascent Technology solution to your specific rules, constraints, and workflows. Interfaces to your existing systems — scheduling, HR, payroll, AODB, DCS — are built and tested. User acceptance testing is conducted with your team.

Phase 3 — Deployment & Go-Live

Data migration is completed and the system is deployed to your production environment. Our implementation team provides on-site go-live support to ensure a smooth transition. Post-go-live monitoring and hypercare support follows.

What We Deliver

  • Detailed project plan with milestones and deliverables
  • Configured solution tailored to your operational requirements
  • Integration with your existing IT systems
  • Data migration from legacy systems
  • User acceptance testing support
  • Go-live support and hypercare period
  • Comprehensive user documentation
  • Post-implementation review and optimization

Implementation Details

  • Approach: Three-phase methodology
  • Experience: 38+ years of deployments
  • Delivery: On-site or remote
  • Integration: All major enterprise systems
  • Go-Live: Supervised deployment
