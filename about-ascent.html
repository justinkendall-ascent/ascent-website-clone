About Us

About Ascent Technology

Founded at MIT in 1986, Ascent Technology has spent nearly four decades solving the world's most complex resource allocation, optimization, scheduling, management, and deployment challenges for mission-critical and labor-intensive environments.

Our Story

Ascent Technology, Inc. was founded in 1986 by Professor Patrick Henry Winston and other members of the Artificial Intelligence Laboratory of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) — the laboratory Professor Winston directed for 25 years.

From its origins in the MIT AI Lab, Ascent Technology has applied advanced computational intelligence — including mathematical optimization, constraint-based reasoning, and machine learning — to solve the resource allocation and workforce management challenges faced by the world's most demanding operational environments.

Today, Ascent Technology develops and markets the SmartAirline Operations Center®, SmartAirport Operations Center®, and Ascent WorkZone® — three market-leading solutions that help airlines, airports, and other organizations deploy their costly resources as efficiently, effectively, and economically as possible.

Our Mission

Ascent Technology's mission is to help organizations in mission-critical and labor-intensive environments determine which resources they need, when they need them, and where they need them — so they can operate efficiently, effectively, and at lowest cost.

We accomplish this through our flagship products — SmartAirline, SmartAirport, and WorkZone — and through comprehensive implementation, training, and support services that ensure our customers achieve maximum value from their investment.

Technology Heritage

Ascent Technology's solutions are built on a foundation of rigorous computational science developed over nearly four decades. Our core algorithms combine:

  • Mathematical optimization and operations research
  • Constraint-based scheduling and planning
  • Heuristic and metaheuristic search techniques
  • Machine learning and predictive analytics
  • Real-time decision support and alerting
