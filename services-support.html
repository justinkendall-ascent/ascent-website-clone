Support Services

Customer Support Services

Two levels of support services help users utilize Ascent Technology solutions productively and worry-free. Our support team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

24×7 Support You Can Count On

Because our customers operate in mission-critical environments — airlines, airports, hospitals, casinos — where operations never stop, our support team is always available. Ascent Technology provides comprehensive remote support via telephone, email, web conferences, and internet connectivity.

Support Services Include

  • 24×7 remote support via telephone and email
  • Web conference and screen-sharing support sessions
  • Internet-based remote diagnostic and repair
  • Software maintenance — product updates, patches, and releases
  • Documentation updates and release notes
  • Proactive notification of scheduled maintenance windows

Standard Support

Covers all product-related questions, defect resolution, and access to software updates and patches. Our standard support team provides coverage during extended business hours with after-hours escalation for critical issues.

Premium 24×7 Support

Full around-the-clock coverage for mission-critical operations that cannot tolerate downtime. Premium support includes guaranteed response time SLAs, a dedicated support contact, and priority escalation to senior engineers.

Software Maintenance

All Ascent Technology support contracts include comprehensive software maintenance — ensuring your system is always running the latest, most stable version of our products with all security patches applied.

  • Regular product updates with new features and improvements
  • Security patches and vulnerability fixes
  • Compatibility updates for OS and database platforms
  • Release notes and migration guides for each update

Support Details

  • Coverage: 24×7×365
  • Channels: Phone, email, web conference
  • Levels: Standard & Premium
  • Updates: Software maintenance included
  • Response: SLA-based for Premium
